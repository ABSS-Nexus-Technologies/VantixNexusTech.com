COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

Helpful context on how we kick off engagements, protect confidentiality, and collaborate across borders.

How quickly can we start?

Most discovery and roadmapping sprints can be initiated within one to two weeks following alignment on scope and contract finalization.

Do you sign Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)?

Yes. We are accustomed to executing bilateral NDAs prior to in-depth technical discussions to protect proprietary ideas and codebases.

How do you collaborate across time zones?

Our team operates with overlapping working hours for North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific, maintaining responsive asynchronous communication.

Do we own 100% of the intellectual property?

Yes. Upon project completion and payment, all custom code, design system files, documentation, and assets are fully owned by your company.
