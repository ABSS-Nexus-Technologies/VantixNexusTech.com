FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers regarding Data Analytics & Business Intelligence.

Everything you need to know about technical scope, timelines, deliverables, and integration requirements.

Which data warehouse do you recommend: Snowflake, BigQuery, or Redshift?

The ideal choice depends on your existing infrastructure. BigQuery is seamless for GCP users, Redshift for AWS-native stacks, and Snowflake provides outstanding multi-cloud flexibility and concurrency management.

Can you connect custom databases and niche SaaS software?

Yes. We build custom API connectors and ingestion workers to pull data from internal databases, third-party APIs, and legacy software into your central warehouse.

How do you ensure data security in analytics warehouses?

We enforce column-level encryption, row-level access permissions, automated data masking for PII, and strict audit logging so users only access authorized metrics.

How frequently does data update in the dashboards?

Pipelines can be configured for near real-time streaming updates, hourly syncs, or daily batch refreshes based on your operational requirements and budget.
