PROGRAM DETAILS

Frequently asked questions.

Clear information about working modes, compensation terms, certificate criteria, and selection workflows.

Is this internship program fully remote?

Yes. All internship activities, discussions, project sprint meetings, and mentorship check-ins are conducted remotely through digital collaboration tools.

Are the internships paid?

Yes, our internship opportunities are paid. Exact compensation details, stipend figures, and milestone structures will be confirmed by Nexora Nexus during candidate evaluation and intake.

What are the completion certificate criteria?

A verified completion certificate is awarded subject to stated eligibility requirements, attendance during scheduled syncs, and the satisfactory completion of assigned sprint deliverables.

How long does an internship sprint last?

Program durations are flexible and project-based. Typical engagements range between 4 to 12 weeks depending on student availability, semester schedules, and project milestone scope.

Are these role cards active job vacancies?

The listed cards represent proposed internship categories for interest registration. Active opening dates, sprint intake periods, and capacity are verified by Nexora Nexus on an ongoing basis.

What happens after I submit my application?

Our engineering mentors review submissions periodically. If an opening matches your skills and background, our team will reach out directly to schedule an introductory video conversation.
